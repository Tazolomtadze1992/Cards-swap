# Glossary prototype handoff

Source: Figma `YqyCMj54On5xNGIHkZ1t6k`, node `570:2514`.

The page is available at `/prototypes/glossary` and reuses the homepage header. Its right-side controls remain sticky on desktop and move above the results on narrow screens.

Letters are immediate multi-select filters: choosing several letters shows the union of their word groups, and choosing an active letter again removes it. Search works within the selected letters or across the entire glossary when no letters are active. The local clear button resets only search, while the global clear action resets search and letters together. Both controls update the live result count.

The Georgian glossary entries are temporary prototype content and need editorial approval before production handoff. The search and reset icons are exact exported Figma assets.
