# Article prototype handoff

Source: Figma `YqyCMj54On5xNGIHkZ1t6k`, node `681:6136`.

The protected article detail page is available at `/prototypes/articles`. Article-type cards on the Resources page open it. The header, typography, colors, spacing, exact Figma article images, and existing resource-card component are reused across the prototype.

Recommended content adapts to its item count: two cards are centered, three fill one row, and four or more wrap through the same three-column grid. The default page demonstrates five items. Review the other states at `/prototypes/articles?recommended=2` and `/prototypes/articles?recommended=3`; these query parameters are prototype-only and do not add visible controls.

The Georgian article copy and destinations are temporary. “გამოტოვე სტატია” jumps to recommendations, recommended cards lead to the Resources page, and “სცენარზე გადასვლა” returns to the homepage learning section until the scenario page is designed.
